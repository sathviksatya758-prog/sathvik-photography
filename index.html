03 · About

I'm Katnam Sathvik — a photographer who believes everything breathes.

Rooms, faces, the long pause between a stranger and the camera. My work lives in the seam between documentary and memory.

I shoot what the day gives me — wildlife at dawn, monuments held in slow light, humans at the edge of a quiet thought. My approach is patient. I work primarily with film and take my time.

Most of my best frames happen inside five minutes of real conversation, not direction. Available for editorial commissions, intimate weddings, and personal essay projects.
